How it works

Three steps per bank. Nothing to install on your phone's banking apps, nothing to forward.

  1. 1

    Turn on email alerts in your bank app

    Most Nigerian banks already send one. Make sure email notifications are on for the account you want to track.

  2. 2

    Switch the bank on in WebSpend

    Sign in with the inbox that receives the alerts, then flip the switch for that bank. Tracking starts from that moment, never earlier.

  3. 3

    Every alert becomes a transaction within minutes

    Amount, date, account and balance are read from the alert. You add the category and a short description of your own.

What gets counted

Money is logged only when it crosses the edge of your own accounts. Moving money between accounts you own is not spending, so WebSpend keeps it out of the totals.

How each kind of movement is logged
Movement Logged as
Company pays into Grey Income
Grey to one of your own accounts Nothing
Between your OPay, Moniepoint, GTBank and UBA Nothing
Your account to someone else's Expense
Someone else's account into yours Income

Transfers to yourself

You keep a list of your own account numbers, including ones you do not track. A debit in one account and a credit of the same amount in another, close together in time or sharing a bank reference, is one transfer and is left out. Any transaction can be re-marked as an expense, income or a transfer to self if WebSpend gets it wrong.

Statement import

Alerts can miss things. Import a statement (CSV or JSON) for any period and WebSpend adds what is missing and skips what it already has.

Naira first, dollars beside it

Amounts are kept in naira with a US dollar equivalent from an official daily rate. Each transaction keeps the rate from its own day. Set US dollars as the default and the conversion disappears.

Your inbox, your rules

WebSpend reads the inbox you sign in with, and only messages from the banks you switch on. Nothing before you switch a bank on is ever read.

  • Read-only mailbox access. WebSpend never sends, moves or deletes email.
  • Only the senders you enable. Messages from any other address are not fetched at all.
  • Nothing from the past. Each bank has a tracking-from time set when you switch it on. Older alerts are ignored, even if WebSpend was offline and is catching up.
  • Real alerts only. An alert counts only when it comes from the bank's own domain and passes sender authentication, so look-alike scam emails never enter the ledger.

One ledger, three screens

The server does the work. The apps show the same data and send your edits back.

  • Mac

    A native app with the summary, transactions, categories, import and accounts in a sidebar.

  • iPhone

    Check what is left to spend and sort new transactions as the alerts come in.

  • Web

    The full dashboard in any browser, with statement import for anything alerts missed.
